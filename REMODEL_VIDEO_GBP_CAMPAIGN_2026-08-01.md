# Remodel Video and Google Business Campaign

Status: production plan. No video or ad is live from this document alone.

## Best Video To Make First

Create one 30 to 45 second vertical video from a real kitchen or bathroom project. Real project footage is more useful for contractor trust than a fully synthetic spokesperson.

Shot list:

1. Wide view of the room before work, with the address and people out of frame.
2. Two close views of the problem being solved.
3. One short work-in-progress clip that does not show unsafe behavior.
4. Wide view of the completed room.
5. Two detail views of the finished work.
6. Final screen: `Free written estimate | 618-581-0676 | All-Pro Metro East Construction`.

Record the city, scope, completion month, customer photo permission, and whether the homeowner approved any quote before publishing. If the city cannot be documented, call it a `Metro East project`.

## Real-Project Voiceover Template

Replace every bracketed field with verified information before recording:

> This [kitchen/bathroom] in [verified city] needed help with [documented starting problem]. The project scope included [documented work]. Here is the room before, during, and after the work. Planning a remodel in Belleville, O'Fallon, or the Metro East? Call All-Pro at 618-581-0676 or request a free written estimate online.

Do not use this template if the starting problem, work, city, or result cannot be supported by the project record.

## Synthesia Use

Synthesia is optional. Use it for a clearly labeled planning explainer, not a fake owner appearance, customer testimonial, or Google Business verification video.

Suggested 35-second script:

> Planning a kitchen or bathroom remodel in Belleville or O'Fallon? Start with scope before finishes. Decide what stays, what moves, and whether plumbing, electrical work, ventilation, waterproofing, or hidden repairs belong in the plan. All-Pro reviews the actual room and provides a written estimate based on the selected scope. Send a photo or call 618-581-0676 to start.

On-screen label: `All-Pro homeowner planning guide`.

## Publishing Matrix

| Channel | Format | Destination |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Business Profile | 30-45 second real-project video or planning graphic | Matching city/service page with `utm_source=google_business_profile` |
| Facebook | Vertical video plus short caption | Matching page with `utm_source=facebook` |
| Nextdoor business page | Real local proof or homeowner planning tip | Matching page with `utm_source=nextdoor` |
| YouTube | Short with factual title and city only when verified | Matching page with `utm_source=youtube` |

Use campaign `remodel_focus_202608` and a content value such as `kitchen_belleville_video` so GA4 and lead records can identify the source.

## Important Google Distinction

A Google Business verification video must be a real, continuous recording that follows Google's current instructions. A Synthesia or edited marketing video is not a substitute. Marketing videos can be published after profile access and media features are available.

Do not add `VideoObject` schema until the final video has a stable public URL, thumbnail, upload date, title, and factual description.
