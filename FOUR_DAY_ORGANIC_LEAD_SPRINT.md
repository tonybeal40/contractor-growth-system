# Four-Day Organic Lead Sprint

Run dates: July 30 through August 2, 2026

Goal: create five qualified, permission-based inquiries for kitchen or bathroom projects in Belleville or O'Fallon. This is an operating target, not a ranking or lead guarantee.

Primary landing page:

https://allprometroeastconstruction.com/kitchen-bath-project-review-belleville-ofallon.html

## Daily Publishing Plan

| Day | Focus | Owned channels | Suggested time |
|---|---|---|---|
| July 30 | Belleville kitchen project review | Google Business Profile, Facebook, Nextdoor | Publish as soon as the page is live |
| July 31 | O'Fallon bathroom project review | Google Business Profile, Facebook, Nextdoor | 8:15 AM Central |
| August 1 | Belleville bathroom repair and remodel planning | Google Business Profile, Facebook, Nextdoor | 9:00 AM Central |
| August 2 | O'Fallon kitchen layout and scope planning | Google Business Profile, Facebook, Nextdoor | 6:30 PM Central |

Yelp does not provide the same general publishing API. Add the matching update manually only if Yelp for Business offers an appropriate post/update field.

## Response Standard

1. Check Gmail and the All-Pro lead sheet at 10:00 AM, 2:00 PM, and 6:00 PM.
2. Call a qualified inquiry as soon as practical. If the call is missed and the homeowner consented to project contact, send one short text identifying All-Pro and asking for a good callback time.
3. Record the owner, next action, and follow-up date on the Follow Up Board before moving to the next inquiry.
4. Do not add anyone to marketing unless the separate marketing checkbox is selected.
5. Ask for the address and useful room photos only after the homeowner has requested project contact.

## Organic Distribution Rules

- Use the prepared post for the correct city and date.
- Post only from All-Pro-owned profiles or in community spaces that permit local business posts.
- Do not claim limited openings, guaranteed pricing, guaranteed rankings, or work that All-Pro did not perform.
- Do not reuse the kitchen inspiration image as project proof.
- Do not scrape private profiles or send unsolicited bulk messages.
- Answer useful public questions without hiding that the reply comes from All-Pro.

## Proof and Review Work

Ask up to three past customers for an honest review using:

https://allprometroeastconstruction.com/review

Do not offer an incentive, filter by expected rating, or write the review for the customer. A specific, genuine review that names the city, project type, problem solved, and customer experience is stronger evidence than generic praise.

## Measurement

The landing page and every prepared channel link carry campaign and source fields. Count a lead only when a real homeowner provides usable project contact information and requests follow-up. Public posts, profile views, clicks, and anonymous questions are opportunities, not leads.

At the end of August 2, compare:

- qualified inquiries by city and service
- source and campaign values in the Leads sheet
- calls from the tracked campaign period
- landing-page sessions and form starts in analytics
- response time and contact outcome

Keep the winning city/service message. Rewrite or stop the messages that produce traffic without qualified inquiries.
