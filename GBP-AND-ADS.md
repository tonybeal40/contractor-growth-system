# Google Business Profile and Remodel Campaign

Use this as the source of truth for organic Google Business Profile posts and future paid remodel campaigns. No ad is active merely because copy exists here.

## Proof Rules

- Use a city name with a project photo only when an estimate, invoice, work order, or approved customer record confirms that city.
- Publish a customer quote only when it is a real public review or the customer gave written permission.
- Confirm schedule availability with Bill before publishing any opening, start-date, or scarcity claim.
- Do not publish licensing, insurance, warranty, guarantee, rating, or project-result claims without current documentation.
- When the project location is not confirmed, label the image `Metro East project example`.
