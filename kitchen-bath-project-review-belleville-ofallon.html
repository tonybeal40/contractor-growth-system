Questions before you start

Kitchen and bathroom project review FAQ

What do I need to send?

Your name, phone number, project city, the main problem or change you want, and your preferred timing. One room photo is optional but often helps.

Is this a final estimate?

No. It helps All-Pro understand the request and confirm the useful next step. Final scope and pricing require review of the actual room, selections, access, site conditions, and applicable trade or permit requirements.

Can I submit if I am still researching?

Yes. Choose "Just researching" for timing and explain the decision you need help making. There is no obligation to schedule work.

Do you review projects outside Belleville and O'Fallon?

All-Pro also reviews requests from Shiloh, Swansea, Fairview Heights, Edwardsville, Glen Carbon, and nearby Metro East communities. Availability depends on the address, project scope, and schedule.
